Green does not always mean weed.

ExG detects green vegetation, including cotton. Exclude crop plants before saving. These are cover-based estimates; yellow or dead weeds, crop overlap, and shadows can affect results.

Start a new trial

Normalized ExG = (2G − R − B) / (R + G + B). Pixels above this threshold count as green. One fixed threshold is used for every photo in a trial. 0.10 is a starting value, not a validated threshold.

How ratings work

Untreated only: control = 100 × (1 − plot cover / untreated cover).

Both references: control = 100 × (untreated cover − plot cover) / (untreated cover − weed-free cover).

Weed-free only: the app reports cover difference, without a percentage control rating. Multiple reference photos are averaged equally. Repeat photos are averaged per plot, treatment, and replicate.

Use references from the same date and comparable crop stage. Excluding rows measures visible weeds between rows, not weeds hidden beneath the crop.

Install & keep your data

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: use the browser’s Install app option.

Photos and records are saved on this device. Export results regularly. Clearing browser data removes saved trials; CSV exports do not include photos. The app works offline after its first successful load.
